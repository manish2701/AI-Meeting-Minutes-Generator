01 / Project Overview

Why Automatic Meeting Minutes?

The Problem

Meeting transcripts contain rich discussion but are hard to scan. Manual minutes take time and miss key points, decisions, and action commitments.

The Approach

Fine-tuned sequence-to-sequence models (BART-large-CNN, FLAN-T5-small) extract structured minutes from transcripts — key points, decisions, actions, deadlines.

The Benefit

Structured output (not just text) with speaker-attributed action items and dated deadlines — ready for team follow-up.

07 / Findings

What the Results Show

Transcription Quality

AssemblyAI universal-3-5-pro achieves high language confidence (~0.98) with 81 segments for MTG_32040. Whisper (faster-whisper) produces 31 segments with full speaker mapping. Both capture speaker dialogue.

Model Output Quality

BART and FLAN-T5 both produce 5 key points, 1 decision, 1 action item, and 1 date from MTG_32040. The outputs preserve the meeting's core discussion: kitchen automation, home-vs-restaurant focus, and the follow-up meeting reference.

Structure vs. Raw Text

Generated minutes are not free-form paragraphs — they are organized into Key Points, Decisions, Action Items, and Dates, making them immediately usable for team follow-up.

Limitations Observed

Some decisions and action items are marked “No explicit...” because the transcript is conversational rather than outcome-driven. Long transcripts require truncation for model input. Speaker attribution depends on diarization accuracy.

08 / Limitations & Next Steps

Current Limits & Future Scope

Current Limitations

  • Summary quality depends on transcript clarity.
  • Long transcripts require input-length truncation.
  • Model inference requires GPU/CPU resources (bart_model.pt 1.6 GB).
  • Speaker attribution relies on diarization (not manually corrected).
  • Action-item extraction works best when commitments are explicit.

Future Improvements

  • Evaluate both BART and FLAN-T5 with automated metrics (ROUGE, BERTScore).
  • Extend to multi-language meeting corpora.
  • Integrate with realtime transcription streams.
  • Build a web API (FastAPI/Flask) for live inference.
